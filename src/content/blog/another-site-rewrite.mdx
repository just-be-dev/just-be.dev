I've spent over a decade of my career building web products and yet I find it incredibly difficult to build a site for myself. I'm certainly not alone in this phenomenon. Across the web you'll find a plethora of personal sites with fancy tech and few posts. Usually there will be one like this, explaining why they've written for the nth time. There's almost this Sisyphean quality to personal sites where the desire to tinker brings one back to the beginning again and again while the actual _content_ of the site never gets developed. And so here I am, with the boulder back at the bottom of the hill. A fresh perspective and a renewed commitment for creating a meaningful place for myself on the web.

I've learned over my career that constraints breed creativity. So here are mine:

- Keep it simple
- Use Markdown for content
- Be minimal in design
  - Use a single font family and size
  - Use a simple color palette

The design choices I've made are as important if not more than any technical choices. I'm using a monospace font to mimic a TUI
