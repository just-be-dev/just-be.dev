## Motivating Idea

Imagine a world where every person had their own personal data store. They defined their own shape of the world by how they expressed their data. Some people would have the notion of `contacts` while others have the notion of `people`. These entities in their stores would have different properties with varying formatting. In such a world, how would people send data back and forth if their views of the world were so disperate? How would they map meaning from the outside world to their personal view of the world?

That's the scenario this research aims to explore.
